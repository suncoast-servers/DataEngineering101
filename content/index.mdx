## What this is

**Data Engineering 101** is a curriculum you can follow at your own pace. Everything is **free and open source** (MIT). Hands-on labs use **Docker Compose** on your laptop or a **VPS** (for example Hetzner).

## How to use it

1. Complete [Path 0 — Setup & literacy](/path-00-setup/welcome/).
2. Follow the paths in order in the sidebar (or see the [curriculum outline](https://github.com/TheDevWhoSaysNI/DataEngineering101/blob/main/outline.md)).
3. Run labs from [`labs/compose/`](https://github.com/TheDevWhoSaysNI/DataEngineering101/tree/main/labs/compose) in this repo.
4. Keep a **portfolio repo** using our [template](https://github.com/TheDevWhoSaysNI/DataEngineering101/blob/main/templates/portfolio-readme.md).
