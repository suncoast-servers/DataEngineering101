## Why

YouTube has almost every data engineering topic—but almost no **order**. This project is the order: paths, labs, checks, and portfolio habits.

## Concept

- **Paths** group topics from absolute beginner to capstones.
- **Labs** in `labs/compose/` give you Postgres, object storage, and more—same files on your PC or on a VPS.
- **AI** is encouraged for speed, but you still **run, read, and explain** the work.

## Do

1. Fork or star the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/TheDevWhoSaysNI/DataEngineering101).
2. Create a personal learning repo (name it whatever you like) and paste in [`templates/portfolio-readme.md`](https://github.com/TheDevWhoSaysNI/DataEngineering101/blob/main/templates/portfolio-readme.md).
3. Skim the [curriculum outline](https://github.com/TheDevWhoSaysNI/DataEngineering101/blob/main/outline.md) so you see the full journey.

## AI prompt card

> Summarize Path 0 in five bullet points and suggest a 7-day study schedule at 1 hour per day. Ask me my timezone and constraints before finalizing.

## Exercise

Write three sentences in your portfolio README: why you want to learn data engineering, how many hours per week you can study, and your target date for finishing Path 3 (SQL).

## Check

- Can you explain the difference between this site, the GitHub repo, and your portfolio repo?
- Do you know where lab files live in the monorepo?

## Portfolio hook

Commit your updated portfolio README—your first version-controlled learning artifact.
